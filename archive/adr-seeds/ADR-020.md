**Context.** Semantic retrieval materially outperforms lexical, and it requires resources that not every machine in an estate has.

**Decision.** The sanctioned default is the backend every machine can run, shipped as a committed artifact requiring no setup. Better backends are opt-in on capable hosts. The quality gap is **measured and published** rather than elided.

**Consequences.** Retrieval works everywhere on day one. The team knows exactly what the floor costs in recall and can argue about it with numbers.

**Failure of the simpler design.** The predecessor measured F1 of 0.22 for its lexical floor against 0.84 for full rerank, chose the floor deliberately, published both — and then a single gitignore line excluded the committed index, silently reversing the decision for weeks. **The distribution half must be enforced by whatever does the distributing, or the decision is only a preference.**
