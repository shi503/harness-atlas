**Context.** Across the whole comparison corpus, the row for provenance joined to outcome is empty but for one partial mark, and that mark belongs to a system with automatic capture and no evidenced attribution. The predecessor is the mirror image: strong attribution — owner, RACI, hashes, an insert-only audit trail — and **no join**. No decision references the work that implemented it; no work artifact references the decision it acted on, other than by a hand-written cross-reference nothing checks.

**Decision.** Every dispatched unit of work records the decision IDs it acts under, and every decision entry accumulates references to the work that implemented it. Both directions are written by the machinery that already exists — the run-event stream on one side, the decision store on the other — not by hand.

**Consequences.** "What did we decide, what did we build because of it, and did it work" becomes answerable. This is the corpus's stated unclaimed ground.

**Failure of the simpler design.** Hand-written cross-references decay silently and cannot be audited for what was never written. This repository already holds both halves — an inherited decision store and structured run events — which no other system in the corpus does. The join is an integration, not a build.
