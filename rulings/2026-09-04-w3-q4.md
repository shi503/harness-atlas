# Ruling — a coverage mark with no nameable primitive behind it

**Decided** by KD on 2026-09-04, reviewing the vocabulary ledger, as question 4 of eight.

**The question.** Grid cells marked `●` — *named primitive* — where the harvest could find no unit the
vendor actually names.

**The ruling.** **Downgrade to `◐` and attach a dated note saying why.** This is a **correction**, not
a re-score: the underlying reading did not change, only the mark's accuracy about whether a name
exists.

**The stated exception.** Component `0a` Substrate keeps `●` under a convention stated on the page —
the substrate is named by every harness that has one, and the mark reads that, not an authored unit.

**Why the distinction between a correction and a re-score is load-bearing.** A re-score requires
re-reading the source. A correction does not, because it fixes what the corpus said about its own
evidence rather than what the evidence says. Conflating them would either freeze obvious errors until
a full re-read, or license silent rewriting of scores between reads.
