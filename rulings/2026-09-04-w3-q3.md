# Ruling — UNDERCOUNT is flagged, and the profile waits for its next read

**Decided** by KD on 2026-09-04, reviewing the vocabulary ledger, as question 3 of eight put to them
in that session.

**The question.** The ledger harvest found objects a vendor names that the corresponding profile's
primitives section did not record. Should the profile be corrected immediately?

**The ruling.** **Record the object in the ledger and flag the profile `UNDERCOUNT`. The profile is
corrected when it is next re-read, not retro-fitted.**

**Why it matters, and why it keeps mattering.** A profile is a dated read of a source at a version.
Editing it from a later, different investigation makes it a composite of two reads wearing one date —
which destroys the only thing a dated snapshot is good for. The flag records the debt without
falsifying the record.

**Where it has since applied.** This is the rule that governs every drift a deep read finds against
its profile: `content/codex.md`'s eleven hook events against the twelve documented five days later,
and `content/fractal.md`'s claims about an upstream commit that no longer resolves. In both cases the
deep read carries both figures with both dates and the profile is left alone.
