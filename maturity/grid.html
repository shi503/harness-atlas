LoomWarp · framework reference

The Grid

Twelve warp threads, six thread counts. The warp is the structure that makes work possible; each workstream is one weft thread passed across it. Reliability is a property of the warp, not the weft — and fabric tears at its thinnest section.

Why the metaphor is load-bearing

A loom holds two kinds of thread. The warp is strung first — fixed, held under tension, running the length of the fabric. The weft is passed across it, one pass at a time. The warp never becomes the product. It is the structure that makes the product possible, and you cannot weave on a warp you have not strung.

Warp
The twelve layers, read bottom-up — Foundation, Environment, Agent Harness, System Stacks, Capabilities, Context, Workspaces, Workflow Tasks, Trust, IMPROVE, Teams & Agents, Surfaces. Strung once, held under tension, reused by every job. Each carries its own sub-layers, which is where the grade is actually taken.
Weft
A workstream. One bounded unit of work, passed across the warp. The thing that actually gets made.
Count
The maturity stage of each layer, taken as its weakest sub-layer’s. Low count tears under load; high count bears weight.

The claim, precisely: making an individual workstream better is weaving a nicer thread. Making the system reliable means stringing more warp. That is the difference between an AI widget and AI infrastructure.

The two thresholds

Stage 3 → 4 — structure stops being social and becomes mechanical. Columns 1–3 scale with the people who remember them. Column 4 is enforcement: an invariant held at a point the model cannot reach. Teams stall at 3 indefinitely because column 3 feels like progress and costs nothing organizationally. Nobody drifts into 4 — it is a commitment.

Stage 5 → 6 — you need something to learn from. A self-improving layer requires a corpus of evaluated outcomes. Any layer graded 6 while Evidence — 8b, inside Trust — sits at 2 is claiming a learning loop with nothing feeding it.

Bridge

What layer stages imply about the organization

The Grid grades layers — structural completeness. Established AI-native maturity models grade an organization — posture and operating model. Different subjects, same six stages, same threshold, because an org's posture is what its structure permits.
