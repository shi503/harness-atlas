**Context.** The value an orchestrator adds is the parallel-versus-serial sequencing, not the prose around it. LoomWarp's blueprint has 11 declared edges and, as of the last consolidation review, none had ever been exercised.

**Decision.** Every workstream declares `depends_on` in the blueprint. The blueprint's notes carry the human-readable execution waves — the reading of the graph that a reviewer actually checks. Contingent workstreams get a first-class field rather than a prose annotation.

**Consequences.** The decomposition is reviewable before dispatch. Waves make an incorrect graph visible to a human in seconds.

**Failure of the simpler design.** The predecessor expressed contingency in `description` prose because the schema had no field for it, and nothing enforced it. The result is a declared graph that the resolver silently ignores — worse than no graph, because it reads as enforced.
