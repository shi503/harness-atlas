**Context.** Completion claims that are not backed by artifacts are unverifiable, and a missing section is indistinguishable from a section that had nothing to report.

**Decision.** The handoff opens by declaring itself the approval gate and forbidding its own generation while the gate is failing. Work completed cites file paths and symbols. Work **not** completed is written out, and if everything shipped the literal word `None` appears. Technical debt is the same. A PASS/FAIL/N-A table covers every gate command, and numbered reproduction steps close it.

**Consequences.** Every completion claim resolves to an artifact. Skipped sections become visible.

**Failure of the simpler design.** An optional not-completed section is always omitted, because the incentive at handoff time is to look finished. Forcing an explicit negative is the whole mechanism.
