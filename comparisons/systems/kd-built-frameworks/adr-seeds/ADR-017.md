**Context.** Unowned decisions are the failure mode a decision log exists to prevent, and they are invisible unless something checks.

**Decision.** The RACI block is required on every entry, with `responsible` carrying a minimum of one and a maximum of five; accountable, consulted and informed are optional with their own ceilings. All initials must resolve against a people registry. The validator fails a non-conforming entry.

**Consequences.** No decision enters the store without a named owner.

**Failure of the simpler design.** In the predecessor this is the **only** rule anywhere in the system that a machine enforces, and it is the only rule that never degraded across 242 entries. Every prose-only discipline in the same system drifted. That asymmetry is the argument.
