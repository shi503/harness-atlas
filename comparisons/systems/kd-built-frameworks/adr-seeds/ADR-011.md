**Context.** A single review gate either blocks on subjective quality (and stalls) or does not block at all (and lets defects through).

**Decision.** Four layers with distinct owners: (1) deterministic — lint, build, type-check, test, security scan, and diff-scope verification against the declared manifest; (2) LLM judgment — quality, idiom, intent alignment; (3) qualitative — legibility to a newcomer; (4) strategic — does it serve the team. Layers 1 and 2 block a handoff. Layers 3 and 4 advise and are owned by the Tier-0 role. The judgment-layer template carries a **known-false-positives register**.

**Consequences.** Blocking is reserved for checkable things. Strategic review happens without becoming a bottleneck.

**Failure of the simpler design.** A model-graded review with no false-positive register produces noise, and reviewers learn to skip it — which removes the layer without anyone deciding to remove it.
